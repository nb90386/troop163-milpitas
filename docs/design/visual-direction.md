# Visual direction

## Three directions considered

The taste-skill direction draw (`draw.mjs`, sector nonprofit, seed 619430176) dealt three ways of composing. Each was held against what only this troop has: a real, timestamped week at Camp Hi-Sierra (523 photos), a lake full of Scout-flag sails, and a troop that describes itself as Scout-led.

| Direction | What it would have been | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The page is the object** | The site as a printed field journal or camp program: a paper sheet on a dark ground, everything set like a printed artefact. | Charming, but it puts paper in front of the photographs. The brief's top priority is the photography. Rejected. |
| **One motif** | One shape (a pine silhouette, a tent ridge) used as every mask, button and divider. | Strong system, but it pulls toward illustration and decoration, and the brief bans fake adventure graphics. Rejected. |
| **The place carries it** ✅ | One full-bleed photograph of where you'd be standing, the name set large along the bottom edge, large pictures and few words after that. | Chosen. The album has the frames to carry it, and it matches "a premium outdoor publication". |

## The idea

> On this site, **the timestamps of one real week at Camp Hi-Sierra (Sunday July 2 to Saturday July 8, 2023)** become **a day-by-day story the visitor scrolls through**.

The competitor test: another troop can't use this sentence. It's our week, our photos, our times ("flags at 7:48", "somebody was up at 5:22", "packed out by 7:13").

**The live thing:** the day strip in the signature scene (Sun to Sat). It tracks progress as you scroll, and tapping a day jumps the story to it. On phones and with reduced motion, the same seven days become a plain sequence of cards.

## Adjustments to the dealt direction

- **Palette and type kept from the brief** (granite paper, forest, clay; an editorial serif) instead of the dealt warm-black/gold and Switzer. The brief fixes the colour story, and the photographs are full of forest green and sail colours that warm black would have flattened.
- **Clay deepened** from `#C26C46` to `#A9512F` so white button text passes WCAG AA (5.38:1). The original clay survives as the decorative underline tone.
- **The pin is held to about three screens**, as the house rules require: seven cards, with 1px of scroll moving the track about 1.7px.

## Page grammar (home)

1. Hero: the sail photo full-bleed, the wordmark along the bottom edge, one sentence and two actions.
2. Intro: a right-half statement and a lone photo hanging below it.
3. **Signature:** "Seven days at Hi-Sierra", a pinned scene where the aerial forest holds still and the day cards cross it.
4. What you'll actually do: rows hung from one vertical axis, each with an unequal margin photo.
5. Scouts run it: a wide photo, a forest tile with the motto and verified facts.
6. From the trip log: a filmstrip of the Hi-Sierra week, then typographic covers for older trips.
7. Gallery band: photos gathered round a heading, drifting at different rates.
8. Before your first visit: practical facts and an accordion beside a tall photo.
9. Closing: a strip of three photos, then a sage field with one underlined action.
10. Footer: a photo half, a menu half, and the name returning at full width as texture.

Neighbouring sections never share a layout, and none of them is a card grid.
