# Troop 163 / 2163: Source Photo Leads

Research date: 2026-11-14.
- No images were saved to the project. Copies were looked at only in a temporary scratch directory.
- "Actual px" is the real file size. "Display" is the width×height set in the page HTML.
- The upload date comes from the timestamp TroopWebHost adds to each filename (`_YYYYMD…`). It is an inference, not a stated date.
- EXIF capture dates come from the camera metadata in the served files. They are reliable for when a photo was taken but say nothing about where.
- **Youth faces:** most images show identifiable minors. Confirm consent and youth-protection rules before any reuse.
- **Low resolution:** apart from the banner, every TroopWebHost image is only 480 px wide, too small for hero or full-bleed use. Ask leadership for the originals.

Base URL for TroopWebHost images: `https://troopwebhost.blob.core.windows.net/troop2163milpitas/`
