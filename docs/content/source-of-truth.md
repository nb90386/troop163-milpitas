## 3. Troop 163 vs 2163 arrangement (what the evidence says)

- There are two separate troop numbers sharing **one website, one contact email and one sponsor**:
  - S1: "Troops 163 (boys) & 2163 (girls)"
  - S3: "Scouts BSA Troops 163/2163"
- **Troop 163** is the boys' troop, "Founded in 1993" (S1 About). The 2014 Weebly site calls it "a boy run troop" (S2).
- **Troop 2163** is the girls' troop, "established … on March 1, 2019 with 6 scouts" (S1 About). It is "the first all-girl troop in Milpitas" (S5, 2021).
- Both troops share an adult leadership family, as of the 2020–21 council program (S6, HISTORICAL): the Troop 2163 Scoutmaster "helped establish the first Scouts BSA Girls Troop, Troop 2163 in Milpitas, while her husband serves as Scoutmaster of Troop 163, and her son is ASM for Troops 163 and 2163 in Milpitas."
- Both troops use the same weekly meeting slot as stated on S1 ("WEEKLY MEETINGS / Monday's 6:30 - 8:00 PM in Milpitas"). Whether they meet jointly or in separate rooms or patrols is **not stated**.
- The site footer reads "Scout Troop 163 of Milpitas, California". The TroopWebHost image storage is named `troop2163milpitas`, so the account appears to have originated with 2163. That is an inference only.

It was a very hard backing trip since it was the first backpacking trip for a couple of scouts. The hardest part was going up because we were going up three thousand feet in elevation. It was a fun trip."
