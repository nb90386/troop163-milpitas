## Selection reasoning

- **Hero: `sail-lone-boat`.** A Scout-flag sail on open water below a granite ridge. It is the single frame that says "these kids go on adventures", the people in it are too small to identify, and it has calm water where text can sit.
- **Signature scene ground: `forest-from-tower`.** Looking straight down on camp, darkened, so the day cards read over it.
- **One photo per day of the week**, chosen from that day's timestamps so the story is told in true order.
- Night photos (campfires, lodge at dusk, dawn moon) carry the mood; program-area photos show what Scouts do.
- Close-ups of faces were the strongest emotionally (`two-friends`, `lashing-team`, `scout-teaching`), which is why they're kept ready in the review build rather than dropped.
